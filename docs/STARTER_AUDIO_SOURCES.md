## Import constraints in the current product

The current founding catalog accepts original producer work and requires a rights warranty. It rejects redistributed third-party material in sound packs. These CC0 files should not be passed through that form as if recorded or composed by the Soundfuel account owner. A separate, explicitly attributed free/demo import flow and corresponding policy treatment are needed before publishing unchanged third-party files. This research does not change the upload warranty or enable commerce.

For a future approved demo import, retain: original title, creator, source URL, licence URL, download date, source readme, file checksum, any modifications and the intended catalog kind. Do not guess BPM/key, sample counts or cultural provenance. Packages may require extracting an archive and assembling a ZIP; previews must be separately prepared MP3s. The master/ZIP stays private and must not be used as the preview URL.

Avoid using a site's general phrase “royalty-free” as proof of standalone redistribution permission. Permission to make songs with a sample and permission to redistribute the source audio are different grants.
